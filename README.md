# 🛒 Blinkit Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Power BI Dashboard** built to analyze Blinkit's sales and profit performance across different product categories. The dashboard provides a clear view of revenue generation, profitability trends, and category-wise performance, helping stakeholders make data-driven business decisions. Similar Blinkit analytics projects often focus on sales performance, category insights, and KPI tracking for decision-making. ([GitHub][1])



## 🎯 Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and profit performance.
* Identify top-performing product categories.
* Compare revenue and profit contribution across categories.
* Provide interactive filtering for deeper business insights.
* Support data-driven decision-making through visualization.



## 📊 Dashboard Features

### KPI Cards

* **Total Profit:** 10.58K
* **Total Sales Value:** 85.95K
* **Average Sales Analysis**

### Interactive Filters

Users can dynamically filter the dashboard by:

* Profit
* Delivery Date
* Price Per Unit

### Visualizations

1. **Profit by Category (Line Chart)**

   * Displays profit trends across categories.

2. **Profit Distribution (Donut Chart)**

   * Shows category-wise contribution to total profit.

3. **Sales by Category (Bar Chart)**

   * Compares total sales generated by each category.

4. **Total Price by Category (Funnel Chart)**

   * Highlights category-wise revenue contribution.

5. **Profit Comparison (Column Chart)**

   * Provides an easy comparison of profits among categories.



## 🔍 Key Insights

* **Beverages** generate the highest profit and revenue.
* **Snacks** are the second-best performing category.
* **Personal Care** contributes the least profit among analyzed categories.
* Sales and profit are concentrated in a few high-performing categories.
* Category-wise analysis helps identify areas for growth and optimization.
